US State Department warned that payment to Iran for safe passage exposes carriers to sanctions. ([Al Jazeera, May 18](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/5/18/iran-plans-to-offer-insurance-for-hormuz-transit-will-it-work); [Bloomberg, May 18](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-18/iran-starts-bitcoin-backed-shipping-insurance-for-hormuz-strait))

([Insurance Journal, May 19](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/05/19/870495.htm))
- **Brent eased from ~$114 (May 5) → ~$105 (May 18), then re-spiked ~5.4% on May 19 to ~$111** on Trump rejection of Iran's peace proposal. Morgan Stanley Q2 forecast $110/bbl. The twelve-days-of-ease vs one-day-re-spike asymmetry supports Iran-talks optionality as the dominant marginal driver in the window. ([Capital.com, May 19](https://capital.com/en-int/market-updates/crude-oil-price-forecast-19-05-2026))
- **India's April crude import mix has the Russia line peaking and then giving back ~20%.** April Russian volume was ~1.57 mb/d (down from March's ~2.0 mb/d after the reported early-April waiver expiry), but May 2026 projections from Kpler put Russia back at ~2.1 mb/d (a record monthly high, ~40-47% of total imports). Secondary-market re-routing through non-USD settlement and alternative shipping/insurance chains continues to support flow; waiver-dependence is not the binding constraint. Hardeep Puri (CII Annual Summit, May 13) put India's combined crude reserve cover at "approximately 60 days" (public-comms framing alongside the underlying 9-13 days strategic / ~74 days commercial breakdown). ([Discovery Alert, May 2026](https://discoveryalert.com.au/india-russian-oil-imports-middle-east-crisis-hormuz-2026/); [Discovery Alert / Puri](https://discoveryalert.com.au/india-crude-lng-reserves-hormuz-energy-security-2026/))

**Net read:** Not a routine biweekly. The "system has already broken" framing on the May 5 dashboard has hardened: the strait is institutionalising in its broken state. Two institutional moves in 48 hours at different evidence tiers — Iran at announcement tier, Chubb at regulated-balance-sheet tier — converge on the same direction with different evidence weight.

### Hormuz / Gulf situation news since 2026-05-05

**Three substantive escalation events in the May 5 → May 20 window:**

1. **May 13, 2026 — Indian-flagged cargo ship "Haji Ali" struck and sunk off Oman.**
   - Vessel: Haji Ali, registered at Salaya Port (Devbhoomi Dwarka, Gujarat). Sailing from Somalia.
   - Hit by unknown projectile (suspected drone); explosion + fire; total loss.
   - All 14 crew rescued by Omani authorities — no fatalities.
   - No actor claimed responsibility publicly.
   - Among the most serious India-flag incidents in the window since Feb 28.
   - Sources: [The Week, May 14](https://www.theweek.in/news/middle-east/2026/05/14/oman-authorities-rescue-crew-aboard-indian-flagged-ship-targeted-near-hormuz-mea-calls-attack-unacceptable.html); [news24, May 14](https://news24online.com/world/indian-cargo-ship-haji-ali-sinks-after-suspected-drone-attack-in-strait-of-hormuz-india-calls-incident-unacceptable/836967/); [Discovery Alert](https://discoveryalert.com.au/strait-hormuz-india-flagged-vessel-attack-2026-oman/)

2. **May 18, 2026 — Iran launched "Hormuz Safe" Bitcoin-backed insurance + Persian Gulf Strait Authority (PGSA).**
   - Announced via semi-official Fars news agency.
   - Iran projects $10bn+ annual revenue from insurance + transit fees.
   - Settlement in Bitcoin to circumvent dollar sanctions; "encrypted verification capability" for vessels.
   - PGSA positioned as the regional authority providing "real-time updates" on operations.
   - **Tier:** announcement (regime launched; operational mechanics not yet proven).
   - US State Department response (May 18-19): "international waterways must remain open … no country should impose unilateral tolls on transit passage"; carriers paying Iran face sanctions exposure.
   - Sources: [Al Jazeera, May 18](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/5/18/iran-plans-to-offer-insurance-for-hormuz-transit-will-it-work); [Bloomberg, May 18](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-18/iran-starts-bitcoin-backed-shipping-insurance-for-hormuz-strait)

3. **May 18-19, 2026 — Chubb publicly reported as joining a $20bn US-supported maritime reinsurance pool.**
   - Pool offers war-risk cover for hulls, cargo, and liability for vessels transiting Hormuz.
   - First major reinsurer publicly reported to join the pool in the May 18-19 announcement window.
   - **Tier:** regulated-balance-sheet (pool membership in a regulated vehicle, not yet a record of underwritten transit).
   - Source: [Insurance Journal, May 19](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/05/19/870495.htm)

## What we are not claiming

- That Iran's Hormuz Safe regime is operationally functioning. It was announced May 18; mechanics unproven.
- That Chubb has underwritten transit. The Insurance Journal source supports pool membership, not yet a record of underwritten transit.
- That Brent's path is single-factor causal. The May 5-18 ease is consistent with Iran-talks optionality, US shale supply-elasticity, and demand cooling all in the price. The May 19 re-spike is consistent with the talks dimension as the dominant driver in the window — author analysis, not market consensus.
- That India's "approximately 60 days" Puri figure is the authoritative number. It is the line GoI is using publicly; underlying breakdown is 9-13 days strategic / ~74 days commercial.
